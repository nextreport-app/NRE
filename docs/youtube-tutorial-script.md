# NextReport — YouTube Tutorial Script

**Title:** How to Generate a Meta Ads Client Report in 5 Minutes | NextReport Tutorial  
**Length:** ~8–10 minutes  
**URL to show:** https://nextreport.in

---

## Before recording (you)

1. Create a demo account or use your admin account.
2. Prepare a **sample Meta CSV** (no real client names — blur if needed).
3. Record at 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, dark mode off (site is dark already).
4. Upload to YouTube with chapters below.

---

## Intro (0:00 – 0:30)

**On screen:** nextreport.in homepage

**Say:**
> If you run Meta Ads for clients, you know the pain — download CSV, copy numbers into PowerPoint, write summaries, fix formatting. NextReport automates that. Upload a CSV, get a branded PowerPoint or Google Slides report with AI-written insights in minutes. Let me show you the full workflow.

---

## Sign up / log in (0:30 – 1:00)

**On screen:** /signup or /login

**Say:**
> Start with a free 7-day trial — no credit card. Sign up with email or Google.

**Do:** Log in to dashboard.

---

## Add a client (1:00 – 1:45)

**On screen:** /clients → Add client

**Say:**
> Each client gets their own workspace — set name, currency, timezone, and optional monthly budget. Upload their logo for the cover slide.

**Do:** Create or open an existing demo client.

---

## Download CSV from Meta (1:45 – 3:00)

**On screen:** Meta Ads Manager (or skip with “you already have this file”)

**Say:**
> In Meta Ads Manager, go to Reports, pick your date range, use a daily breakdown, export campaigns and ad sets. NextReport accepts CSV, Excel, or TSV — it auto-detects columns.

**Optional:** Show /help/download for the exact steps.

---

## Upload wizard (3:00 – 5:30)

**On screen:** New report wizard

**Say:**
> Click New Report, upload your MTD Daily CSV. NextReport detects Meta vs Google automatically. Step two — pick which campaigns to include. Step three — choose your weekly window; month-to-date is calculated automatically. Step four — preview slides and metrics. Step five — generate.

**Do:** Walk through all 5 steps, show preview carousel.

---

## Download & share (5:30 – 7:00)

**On screen:** Report complete / download screen

**Say:**
> Download the PowerPoint, or get a Google Slides link if you connected Google Drive. Share a read-only link with your client, email the report, or open WhatsApp share. You can also save to Drive in one click.

**Do:** Download PPTX, copy share link, briefly show share page.

---

**On screen:** /account

**Say:**
> In Account settings you can connect Google Drive, Meta Ads for API access, and Slack or Zapier webhooks — when a report finishes, your team gets notified automatically.
