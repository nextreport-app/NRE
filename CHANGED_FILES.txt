  Looked up by the campaign's own slugified objective key (via the
  already-imported slugifyObjectiveKey), so a key like "frequency" is only
  ever protected for a Reach-objective campaign, never leaking protected
  status onto a different objective's slot 7/8 candidate.

WHY: objectiveMetricKeys(resultLabel) mechanically slugifies the whole
resultLabel to build its synthetic {resultKey, costKey}. For most
objectives that already matches defaultMetaSelection's own real key (the
generic "results"/"cost_per_result", already covered by the existing
fallback strings, or a dedicated key that happens to equal the slugified
form). For META FORM LEADS specifically it never does: costKey computes
to the plural "cost_per_meta_form_leads" (inherited from resultLabel's
own trailing "S"), but defaultMetaSelection (available-metrics.ts) uses
the singular "cost_per_meta_form_lead" (the real meta-dictionary.ts key)
in two of its three CSV-column-dependent branches, or "cost_per_lead"
entirely in the third — matching none of them. That's the exact reported
bug: a META FORM LEADS campaign's slot 5 showing COST PER LINK CLICK
instead of COST PER LEAD, because redistributeCardSlots didn't recognize
the selected metric's own key as the primary pair and treated it as an
ordinary, replaceable secondary. The same class of mismatch exists for
REACH/UNIQUE REACH ("frequency" vs. "cost_per_1k_reached"), LANDING PAGE
VIEWS ("cost_per_lpv" vs. "cost_per_landing_page_views"), and the
MESSAGING objectives ("messaging_conversations_started"/
"cost_per_conversation" regardless of which of the three MESSAGING
labels the campaign resolved to) — found by tracing every branch of
defaultMetaSelection's switch statement against objectiveMetricKeys'
synthetic-key derivation, not by guessing.

WHY THE FIX IS OBJECTIVE-CONDITIONAL, NOT A FLAT LIST: the originally
proposed fix added extra keys as unconditional strings alongside
"results"/"cost_per_result". That would have been wrong for two of the
proposed keys specifically: "frequency" and "cost_per_lpv" are both in
SECONDARY_FILL_KEYS — valid, intentionally-compactable slot 7/8
candidates for OTHER objectives. An existing test (redistributeCardSlots
— "compacts real (non-null) SECONDARY cards forward") selects
"frequency" with campaignObjective: null and asserts it gets compacted
away, not protected. Adding it unconditionally would have made it
permanently sticky on every campaign that happens to have it selected,
not just Reach ones — a new bug in place of the old one. Also dropped
several keys from the originally proposed list that don't correspond to
any real key in the codebase (cost_per_website_lead, cost_per_purchase,
cost_per_install — those objectives already use the generic
"cost_per_result" key, already protected) or that are slot 7/8
candidates never meant to be permanently protected (cost_per_atc,
cost_per_ic — ADD TO CART/INITIATE CHECKOUT are PURCHASES' own secondary
picks, not its primary pair).
